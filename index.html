About me

I spent three years as a Customer Interface Specialist at NAVEX, turning client designs and requirements into live, working web platforms, and was promoted to Senior in 2025. I built customer-branded sites to deadline, configured them across parent and child customer tiers, and tracked down faults on live sites until I could explain the cause and the fix.

Before that I spent seven years in cinema management, and a decade as a projectionist at the Cambridge Film Festival, where I still run the technical testing of every film. I taught myself to code during a furlough, then trained properly at Le Wagon while working full time, and stayed on as a teaching assistant.

I like problems with a clear before and after: an idea, a mockup, a mess of requirements, and then a working thing on the other side of it.
