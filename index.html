Web Intake Site Assistant Tool

A browser tool I built at NAVEX to speed up everyday maintenance work on customer intake sites. The panel can be dragged anywhere on the page and minimised, and it remembers its state between sites. I maintained it on GitHub, presented it to my team, and it was rolled out across the team.
